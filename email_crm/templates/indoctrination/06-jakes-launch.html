🎯 The Foundation That Changes Everything

Before you build another funnel...

Before you write another email...

Before you spend another dollar on ads...


You need to nail down your foundation.

Here are the 3 questions you MUST answer:


Question 1: Who is your SPECIFIC customer?

Not "business owners." Not "entrepreneurs."

Get specific:

  • How old are they?

  • What do they do for work?

  • What are they struggling with RIGHT NOW?

  • What do they want more than anything?

Example:

❌ "I help entrepreneurs make more money."

✅ "I help 30-45 year old consultants who are booked solid but can't scale past $10K/month because they're trading time for money."

See the difference?


Question 2: What is the ONE problem you solve?

Not 5 problems. Not "I do everything."

ONE clear problem that keeps your customer up at night.

Example:

❌ "I help with marketing, sales, business strategy, mindset, and productivity."

✅ "I help service providers get consistent clients without spending 20 hours a week on social media."


Question 3: Why should they choose YOU?

What makes you different from the 500 other people offering something similar?

This isn't about being "better." It's about being different.

Example:

❌ "I'm the best and I work really hard."

✅ "I'm a former corporate recruiter who knows exactly what hiring managers look for. I've been on both sides of the table."
