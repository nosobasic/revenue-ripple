Concrete gap

behavior. It never invokes retention_billing.events.EventHandler. A valid invoice
or subscription event can therefore receive success from that legacy route without
creating rr_retention_evidence. The retention reader is deployed; ordinary paid
coverage is not automatically populated. Admin/lifetime exceptions are independent.

EventHandler previously constructed EvidenceStore with the default LIVE_SCOPE /
live catalog policy even when a sandbox provider was injected. This made a genuine
isolated sandbox settlement fail the exact-catalog policy. Fixed with optional
explicit policy injection and startup scope/secret validation; live default remains.
